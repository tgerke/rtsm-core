## What it does today

Randomization: import a statistician-generated list, activate it behind a
password step-up with a recorded reason, randomize subjects with
concurrency-safe stratified allocation, and deliver each assignment to
[edc-core](https://github.com/tgerke/edc-core)'s RTSM intake with an
append-only transfer log on both sides. Studies that can't be served by a
list can opt into in-app Pocock–Simon minimization: a pure engine pinned by
an R oracle, with an append-only draw record that replays every assignment
exactly.

Supply: sites with site-scoped access, a kit-to-arm map visible only to the
unblinded pharmacist role, and blinded dispensing — subject and site in,
kit number out, with the arm resolved server-side and an append-only
dispense log. Kits import to a depot and move only by shipment: FEFO
composition with a shelf-life floor, blinded site receipt that dispositions
every kit, and threshold-triggered resupply requests that a pharmacist
turns into shipments or dismisses with a reason.

Emergencies: a medical monitor breaks the blind for one subject behind a
password step-up and a recorded reason. The append-only code-break log shows
blinded staff that a break happened, and for whom — never the arm.

## The sibling family

rtsm-core is the fourth application in the clinical-stack family, next to
edc-core (EDC), ctms-core (CTMS), and lims-core (LIMS). The siblings share a
stack, an OIDC provider, and a compliance pattern: append-only regulated
tables, a hash-chained audit trail written by the database, and a DML-only
runtime role.
